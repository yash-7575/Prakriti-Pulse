
Herb Availability in Kerala:
Kerala has a tropical, humid climate ideal for spices and medicinal herbs.
Commonly available herbs: Turmeric (Haridra), Black Pepper (Maricha), Ginger (Ardrakam), Brahmi, Shankhpushpi, and Tulsi.
Ayurvedic treatments in Kerala often focus on Panchakarma and oil massages (Abhyanga) due to the favorable humid climate which helps open pores.

Herb Availability in Maharashtra:
Maharashtra has a varied climate, ranging from the humid Konkan coast to the dry interior (Vidarbha/Marathwada).
Commonly available herbs: Tulsi, Neem, Aloe Vera (Kumari), Shatavari, Adulsa (Vasaka), and Giloy (Guduchi).
In the dry regions, cooling and moisturizing herbs like Shatavari and Aloe Vera are often recommended to balance Vata and Pitta.

Herb Availability in Assam:
Assam has a subtropical climate with heavy rainfall and high humidity.
Commonly available herbs: Turmeric (especially the potent Lakadong variety), Pippali (Long Pepper), Brahmi, and Hibiscus.
The climate favors herbs that balance Kapha and Vata, preventing dampness-related issues.

Herbs for Humid Climates:
In humid climates (like Kerala, Assam, Konkan coast), Kapha dosha can easily get aggravated, leading to congestion and sluggishness.
Recommended herbs: Tulsi, Ginger, Turmeric, Black Pepper, and Triphala. These help dry up excess moisture and boost digestion (Agni).

Herbs for Dry Climates:
In dry climates (like interior Maharashtra, Rajasthan), Vata dosha tends to increase, causing dryness and dehydration.
Recommended herbs: Shatavari, Ashwagandha, Licorice (Yashtimadhu), and Aloe Vera. These are nourishing and moisturizing (Snigdha).

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera):
Dosha Effect: Balances Vata and Kapha.
Benefits: Adaptogen for stress, improves stamina, supports sleep.
Contraindications: Avoid in high Pitta (can cause heat), pregnancy, and congestion.
Availability: Widely available across India, grows well in drier regions like Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan.

Tulsi (Holy Basil):
Dosha Effect: Balances Vata and Kapha. Increases Pitta slightly.
Benefits: Respiratory support, immunity booster, anti-microbial.
Availability: Found in almost every Indian household, grows well in most climates.

Guduchi (Giloy / Tinospora cordifolia):
Dosha Effect: Tridoshic (Balances Vata, Pitta, and Kapha).
Benefits: Powerful immunomodulator, fever reducer, blood purifier.
Availability: Grows as a climber in tropical and subtropical regions (Maharashtra, Kerala, etc.).
