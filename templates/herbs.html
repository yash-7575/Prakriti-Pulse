Understanding Herb Properties

Rasa (Taste): The primary taste of the herb, which affects its therapeutic properties.

Virya (Potency): The heating or cooling effect of the herb on the body.

Vipaka (Post-digestive Effect): The taste that develops after digestion.

Prabhava (Special Action): Unique therapeutic effects beyond the normal properties.
