About the Official Assessment

The standardized Prakriti assessment is conducted by CCRAS-certified practitioners under the Central Council for Research in Ayurvedic Sciences (CCRAS), Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India. This is a validated, research-backed assessment requiring in-person examination.

1

Find an Expert

Visit a designated CCRAS Research Institute or an All India Institute of Ayurveda (AIIA) Center near you.

2

Book Appointment

Schedule a comprehensive physical examination. The session typically lasts 45-60 minutes for a detailed evaluation.

3

The Assessment

A qualified Ayurvedic doctor will evaluate your physical traits, physiological functions, and psychological characteristics.

4

Get Report

Receive your official Prakriti Constitution Certificate, which serves as a baseline for all future Ayurvedic treatments.
