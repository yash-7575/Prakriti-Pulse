Ashwagandha

Withania somnifera

Vata & Kapha Balancing

Known as "Indian Winter Cherry," this adaptogenic herb helps reduce stress, improve sleep quality, and enhance overall vitality.

Benefits:

  • • Reduces stress and anxiety
  • • Improves sleep quality
  • • Boosts energy and stamina
  • • Supports immune function

Dosage:

300-600mg daily with warm milk or water

Best For:

Stress Sleep Energy

Turmeric

Curcuma longa

Pitta & Kapha Balancing

The "Golden Spice" with powerful anti-inflammatory properties, traditionally used for joint health and digestive support.

Benefits:

  • • Powerful anti-inflammatory
  • • Supports joint health
  • • Aids digestion
  • • Boosts antioxidant levels

Dosage:

500-1000mg daily with black pepper for absorption

Best For:

Inflammation Joints Digestion

Ginger

Zingiber officinale

Vata & Kapha Balancing

A warming spice that ignites digestive fire, reduces nausea, and supports circulation throughout the body.

Benefits:

  • • Aids digestion and reduces nausea
  • • Supports circulation
  • • Natural anti-inflammatory
  • • Boosts immune system

Dosage:

1-3g fresh root or 250-500mg powder daily

Best For:

Digestion Nausea Circulation
